Vosem Sans webfont derivative

Vosem Sans is an unhinted derivative of the Lato 3.100 font files retained
in this repository. Original author: Lukasz Dziedzic. The original copyright
and SIL Open Font License 1.1 are in the accompanying Lato-OFL.txt and remain
embedded in the fonts. These derivatives are distributed under that same OFL.
Lato is a Reserved Font Name; the modified fonts use Vosem Sans / VosemSans
as their family, full, PostScript and unique names. This modification is not
an official Lato release and does not imply endorsement by the original author.

Reason for modification:
The original TrueType hints produce thin horizontal breaks in Cyrillic glyphs
(including "a", U+0430) at small browser text sizes. Removing those instructions
retains the original letter shapes while letting the browser rasterize their
continuous outlines. The Regular, Medium and Italic styles are treated equally.

Changes:
- Removed glyph-level hint instructions and font-wide TrueType hint tables.
- Updated hint-related maxp/head settings and requested smooth, unhinted
  rendering at all sizes in gasp.
- Renamed the derived font family and version; retained original attribution.
- Removed the now-invalid digital-signature placeholder.
- Packaged as WOFF2 without glyph/table transforms or subsetting.

Preserved and verified by scripts/compress-fonts.py:
- Every glyph outline and composite transformation, checked using RecordingPen.
- The full glyph order and character map, including Cyrillic.
- Exact advance widths, side bearings, vertical metrics and font styling.
- Byte-identical cmap, hmtx, hhea, OS/2, GDEF, GPOS, GSUB and post tables.
- Original copyright, author, trademark, attribution and license records.

Original Lato TTF and historical WOFF2 files are retained unchanged.

Reproduce with Python 3 and fonttools[woff]:
  python scripts/compress-fonts.py
Historical unmodified WOFF2 packaging remains available with --originals.

Generated with Python 3.12.14, FontTools 4.65.0 and Brotli 1.2.0.
